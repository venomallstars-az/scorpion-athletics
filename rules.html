THE SCORPION STANDARD

GYM EXPECTATIONS.

The standards that keep the Den safe, positive, and successful.

01
Safety

Safety Comes First

Athletes must follow coach directions and gym safety policies at all times.

02
Respect

Respect Everyone

Treat coaches, teammates, staff, families, officials, and visitors with respect.

03
Effort

Give Your Best Effort

Come ready to work, learn, and improve throughout every practice.

04
Coachability

Be Coachable

Listen to corrections, ask questions, and approach feedback with a positive attitude.

05
Attendance

Be Present & On Time

Consistent attendance and arriving on time help the entire team succeed.

06
Teamwork

Put The Team First

Support teammates and contribute to a positive team environment.

07
Gym Care

Respect The Facility

Keep personal belongings organized and help keep the Den clean.

08
Sportsmanship

Compete With Character

Celebrate your teammates, respect competitors, and represent Scorpion Athletics positively.
