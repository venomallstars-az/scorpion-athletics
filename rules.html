SCORPION STANDARD

GYM RULES.

Safety, respect, effort, and teamwork come first.

01

Safety First

Follow coach instructions, use equipment correctly, and never attempt a skill without appropriate coaching.

02

Be Respectful

Treat athletes, coaches, families, officials, and staff with respect.

03

Be Coachable

Listen, ask questions, accept corrections, and give consistent effort.

04

Arrive Prepared

Arrive on time with appropriate practice clothes, secured hair, water, and required equipment.

05

Keep It Clean

Clean up personal belongings and help keep the gym organized.

06

Team First

Encourage teammates and celebrate progress. Compete hard without tearing others down.
