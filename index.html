Athlete Handbook

Gym Rules

01 · Attendance

Athletes are expected to attend scheduled practices and arrive ready to participate.

02 · Respect

Athletes, parents, coaches, and teammates must treat one another with respect.

03 · Practice Attire

Athletes must wear appropriate athletic clothing, cheer shoes, and hair secured away from the face.

04 · Phones

Phones should remain put away during practice unless a coach gives permission.

05 · Competition Behavior

Athletes and families are expected to represent Scorpion Athletics respectfully at all competitions.

06 · Safety

Athletes must follow all coach instructions and never attempt skills without proper supervision.

07 · Communication

Parents should use official gym communication channels for schedule questions and announcements.
