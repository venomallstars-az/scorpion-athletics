TRYOUT PROCESS

What We Look For

Athletes are evaluated based on current skills, performance, coachability, effort, teamwork, and overall team placement.

IMPORTANT

Skill Selection

Elite athletes should be prepared to identify their current tumbling passes and jump combination during the evaluation.

Elite teams: Please select 2 running tumbling passes, 2 standing tumbling passes, and 1 jump combo consisting of one single jump followed by two connected jumps.
